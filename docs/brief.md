# The Today brief

One page per item at the top of Today, written overnight by the `pursue-followup` routine from the list
`py engine/briefs.py owed` gives it. The board shows the header on the Today card (the question and options A to C,
each with its reason) and the whole page behind "Open brief". the owner's rule (2026-10-23): when he opens one, it has
everything he needs to do the thing, with no backstory to dig up and nothing to paste into another window.

File: `Projects/job-search/briefs/<kind>-<id>.md` (the path `owed` gives), rewritten whole when it goes stale.

```
# <Company: role title>, or <person's name>

**Key:** <role:<job id> | person:<contact id> | plan:<job id>, exactly as owed gives it>
**For:** <exactly as owed gives it: the item's next step, or "plan vN">
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Kind:** <plan | interview | move | call | intro | touch>
**Question:** <the one decision, one sentence, in the owner's terms>
**Action:** <the recommended next step as one sentence he could act on now>
**Option A:** <label, 2 to 5 words> | <why, one line> | <act>
**Option B:** <label> | <why> | <act>
**Option C:** <label> | <why> | <act>      (only when a third path is real)
**Recommended:** A

## Why this is on today
- <what made it due: a date, a plan waiting, a reply, an aging posting, a window closing>

## Who <name> is to you
- <the relationship as the record has it: how the owner knows them, first-degree since when, last touch and what it was,
  what they said last time. Keep the categories apart (known personally, first-degree only, shared affiliation,
  hypothesized). Nothing invented; when the record is silent, say so and ask the owner to add a line.>

## Options
<each option in two or three sentences: what happens, what it costs, why A is the recommendation>

## Draft
**Channel:** <LinkedIn message | Email | Text>
**To:** <email address for an email, LinkedIn profile URL for a LinkedIn message>
**Subject:** <for an email>
**Gmail draft:** <the draft's link, when the routine made one>

```text
<the message, ready to send, in the owner's voice>
```

## Then
- <what happens after: the follow-up date, the application, who the board will draft for next>

## Watch out for
- <risks, open questions, anything to check before sending>

## Sources
- [[<vault files this came from>]], <Notion page>, <posting>
```

Rules:

- Options are real alternatives, not A plus two straw men. A is the recommendation unless `**Recommended:**` says
  otherwise. For a plan, the options are the plan's own (`**Option A:**` lines in campaign.md), copied across.
- `act` is what the card's button does, one of: `brief` (open the brief at the draft; use it for "send this"),
  `done`, `later`, `snooze:N` (N days), `next:<step>|<days>` (a new next step N days out), and for a role `sent`,
  `applied`, `promote`, `bench`, `pause`, `close`, `noresp`, `gone` (close as posting removed), and `slotquiet` (hide the open-slot offer for a week). For a plan card the button records the choice; a `bench`,
  `pause` or `close` act there also stages that move.
- Draft section: a relationship note (congratulations, a check-in, a thank-you, a reply to keep warm) is drafted
  every time (the owner, 2026-10-23). Outreach tied to a role is drafted only once he has answered its plan; before that the
  section says what would be drafted. Nothing is ever sent.
- One page. If it runs longer, cut the Options prose first.
- House style: no em dashes, no emojis.
